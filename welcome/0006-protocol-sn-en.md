# 🎭 I. Verification protocol for scenic citizenship

(Description of the scenic citizenship protocol of IMARCH)

## 🌐 1. General principle

Scenic citizenship of IMARCH Githronical is a form of publicly recognised digital subjectivity, based not on biometric data or a state‑issued identity document, but on sustainable identification through actions, publications and responsibility.

👤 The subject of citizenship can be either an individual or an entire conglomerate.  
🎭 One person can be manifested in many hypostases if specialisation is needed.

Therefore, coordination proceeds not by the method of formal vote counting, but by the method of reputation accounting 🏅, which is formed on the basis of clear criteria of the LCR (labour contribution ratio).

The verification protocol is aimed not at confirming the physical person, but at recording:

+ 🔄 continuity of participation
+ ✍️ authorship of actions
+ ⚖️ consent to bear responsibility for the scene

---

## 🧩 2. Basic identification elements

Scenic citizenship is formed through a combination of four elements:

### 🎭 1. Scenic mask
The publicly declared name of the participating subject in the IMARCH ecosystem.

### 🌐 2. Scenic site (persona‑site)
A personal subdomain representing the mask as a stable scene:

+ 📜 description of the role
+ 🎯 areas of interest
+ 🔗 links to external anchors of interests

### 💻 3. GitHub profile
The main anchor of verification:

+ 📂 records the history of actions
+ ✍️ confirms authorship
+ 🔍 ensures public verifiability

### 📖 4. IMARCH Chronicle
An institutional record of the recognition of the mask as a scenic citizen:

+ 📅 date
+ 🔢 version
+ 🔗 links to the site and GitHub
+ 🏷️ participation status

---

## 📊 5. Verification levels

The protocol provides a multi‑level model:

### 1️⃣ Level 1 — Declarative
🔗 Mutual links between the scenic site and the GitHub profile.  
A public declaration of affiliation is recorded.

### 2️⃣ Level 2 — Confirmation by action
📜 A statement is published in the GitHub profile:  
> “This profile represents the scenic citizen [Mask Name] within the IMARCH ecosystem.”

### 3️⃣ Level 3 — Institutional recognition
📖 IMARCH Githronical publishes an entry in the chronicle: recognition of citizenship, the linkage and a hashed version of the record.

---

## 📬 4. Role of contact forms and communication

Contact forms (Zoho Form and similar):

+ ❌ are not a tool for personal identity verification
+ 📩 are used to record contact with the scene

All forms:
+ send messages to the central IMARCH mail circuit
+ contain scene metadata (mask name, source)

---

## 🚫 6. Attitude to biometrics and personal data

The protocol fundamentally does not use biometrics.  
Reasons: ⚖️ legal risks, 🛡️ no necessity, 🔄 inconsistency with the architecture of scenic subjectivity.

---

## 🏆 7. Final definition

Scenic citizenship of IMARCH Githronical is:
> a form of publicly recognised digital identity based on actions, publications and institutional recording of responsibility.

---

## 📚 II. Brief explanation for experts

Main provisions:
1. 🚫 We do not use biometrics.
2. 💻 Identification is built on professional activity.
3. 🌐 The scenic site is a public participation profile in the Game.
4. 📖 Institutional recognition is recorded publicly.
5. ⚖️ Personal data are used only when necessary.
