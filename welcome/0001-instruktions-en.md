## 🪙 Scene Citizenship Protocol
Onboarding into the AntiPandora Mission

---

🎭 What is scenic citizenship?

It is not a passport.  
It is not an account.  
It is publicly recognised digital subjectivity based on actions, publications and responsibility.

You become a citizen of the scene not by biometrics, but by chronicle.  
Every mask, every commit, every subdomain is an act of recognition.

---

🧩 Verification structure

| Element | Purpose |
|--------|------------|
| 🎭 Mask | Public name of the participating subject |
| 🌐 Scene site | Subdomain reflecting the role, interests, connections |
| 🧬 GitHub profile | History of actions, authorship, verifiability |
| 📜 IMARCH Chronicle | Institutional record of recognition |
| 🧷 Levels | Declaration → Confirmation → Recognition |

---

🛡️ Principles

- No biometrics
- No collection of personal data
- Only actions, publications, scenes
