# For those hearing the word “GitHub” for the first time
Why our humanitarian project lives on a platform for programmers

> *Greetings, bearers of unique forms of knowledge. You have found the “Incubator of Constructive Ideas” and see that we use the GitHub platform.

“This is for technical specialists, right?” you might think. That's correct. It is a professional‑level tool, not a toy. And yes, it was created primarily for programmers.

But we are here precisely to break stereotypes. Allow us to explain why this platform is good not only for code, but also for our common cause.*

---

### 🧭 What is GitHub, if explained through our values?

Forget about code for a moment. Imagine an **ideal workshop for jointly creating anything**.

*   **GitHub is our common, constantly growing library.**  

Instead of books, documents, manifestos, templates, and methodologies are stored here. Everything that forms the core of Universal Formology.  

And each document has not only a current version, but also the entire history of its discussions and changes.

*   **It is our title scene in the digital space.** Here everyone can not only express an idea, but also **formulate it as a concrete, polite proposal** to our common rules. This turns chaotic argument into a constructive, structured dialogue.

*   **It is a mechanism of trust and transparency.**  

You always see who, when, and exactly what was proposed. No hints and conjectures. Only honest work with form and content. The entire evolution of our project lies before you like on the palm of your hand.

**In short: GitHub is not about computers. It's about honesty, structure, and collaborative creation.**

---

### ❓ Frequently asked questions (for those far from IT)

#### **“I'm not a programmer. Why do I need this?”**

> You don't program machines. You “program” social and humanitarian projects. Our tools are words, concepts, missions. GitHub gives you a powerful framework for working with precisely that. You will use the same principles as the creator of complex software, but for creating complex, viable ideas.

#### **“Is it difficult? Do I need to learn commands?”**

> No. To start, you don't need to know any commands. You can simply **read**, just like you read any website. You can participate in discussions through a convenient interface similar to a forum. When you want to propose your edit to our manifesto or template, we will guide you by hand through this simple and elegant ritual.

#### **“Why not just use Google Doc or a chat?”**

> Because in Google Doc it's chaos of versions, and in a chat the idea drowns in 5 minutes. Our project is a living, growing organism. We need not just “correspondence”, but a **single, structured space** where we can track the development of each thought, make informed decisions, and see the big picture. Google Doc is a sheet of paper. GitHub is a whole archive with a catalog and storage system.

#### **“What does ‘clone a repository’ mean?”**

> It's the deepest level of participation. It means **creating a complete, living copy of our entire workshop on your computer**. You will be able to work with our materials offline, make your changes in this personal copy, and then – politely propose to us to move your best developments into the common workshop. This is an act of highest trust and co‑creation.

---

### 🚀 How to start? The simple path.

1.  **Create a free account on GitHub.com.** This is your pass to the world of structured collaboration. The technical foundation for the “stage name” in our digital community.

2.  **Just watch.** Go to our project. Browse the files, read the `README.md` (that's our business card), study the folder structure. Get comfortable on the stage as a spectator.

3.  **Start with discussions.** Go to the **“Discussions”** tab. This is our common living room. Here you can ask a question, share a thought, or find like‑minded people without touching the main documents.

4.  **Follow us.** When you feel ready, we will help you take the first step towards full co‑creation: we'll teach you how to formulate your first idea as a clear proposal.
