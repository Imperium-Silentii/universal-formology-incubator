We don't just give you a name. We enter into a **Memorandum of trust and cooperation** with you.

*   **You receive:** Protection of your title name, the right to vote, access to co‑creation tools, a place in our Citizens' Passport.

We are not an arena for wars. We are a workshop for creation.
