## Why onboarding is needed at all

Most digital projects start the user's acquaintance with registration. A person creates an account, gains access to the interface and then tries to figure out what is happening on their own. As a result, participants remain external observers instead of becoming part of a living system.

IMARCH GitChronical is built differently. Here, entering the project is seen as a transition to a new environment of thinking and interaction. Therefore, instead of simple registration, onboarding is used as a sequential entry protocol.

Onboarding sets the structure of the first experience. It transforms the chaos of first contact into a clear path, where the participant gradually understands the meaning of what is happening, gets acquainted with the culture of the project and begins to form their own role.

---

## What is onboarding in the context of GitChronical

Onboarding can be described as a combination of game, ritual and protocol.

The game creates lightness and involvement.
The ritual forms culture and respect for the process.
The protocol provides clear steps and records the result.

This is similar to a tea ceremony. The actions are simple, but their sequence creates a space of meaning. A person does not just perform steps, but enters a state of participation.

In GitChronical, onboarding becomes a mechanism for transitioning from the position of an observer to the position of a subject who acts and leaves a trace in the chronicle.

---

## Why it is needed

The main goal of onboarding is not technical, but cultural.

It is needed so that:

- the participant goes through the path of a candidate, not just becomes a user
- donors and experts understand the structure of the project and their role within it
- the system immediately forms a unified language of communication and thinking
- instead of chaotic discussions, a culture of argumentation and joint analysis appears.

The project is built around the idea of a chronicle. This means that not only the result is important, but also the path. Onboarding records the participant's first steps and turns them into part of the common history.

---

## What does the entry process consist of

Onboarding is a sequence of stages, each of which reveals a new level of understanding.

First, the participant gets acquainted with the basic materials. These are landing articles explaining the meaning of the project, its structure and principles of operation. At this stage, it is important not to give all the information at once, but to form a sense of direction.

The next layer is related to understanding the key elements of the system: the archive, the collegium, the consilium and the verdict. These concepts describe not the interface, but the cultural logic of interaction. The participant begins to see the project as an institutional environment, not as a set of pages.

Next, the X‑mask profile and X‑mask site appear. This is not just a technical page, but a space for a personal chronicle. Here questions, reflections, stages of passage are recorded. The X‑Mask subject becomes a mirror of the process.

Throughout the journey, the participant is accompanied by an instructor. Their task is not to control, but to help reveal the meaning of the steps and open new levels of access.

---

## What tools are used

On the technical side, onboarding relies on familiar tools, but their meaning is expanded.

GitHub is used as a workshop. Texts, ideas, questions, drafts are stored here. The history of changes turns into a chronicle of development.

Issues and comments allow you to record decisions, discuss the choice of name, ask questions and receive feedback. This is not just a task system, but a space for dialogue.

The subdomain or mask becomes the candidate's personal diary. It shows not only the final result, but also the path to it.

The Constitution and protocols are opened after passing the basic stage. They set the rules of the game for those who become full participants in the system.

---

## How the participant's path goes

The process starts small. The participant is invited to go through short materials that set the general taste of the environment.

If this is enough and the participant wishes to play for the donors' guild without institutional obligations, the onboarding path ends.

For those who accept the condition of entering the body of the institutional Game, the further path becomes more personal. Questions, resonances, the need for choice and formulation of one's own position appear. This is consolidated by the voluntary choice of the status of a candidate for scenic citizenship of IMARCH Githronical | Chronicle OS.

The candidate assigns the stage name of the chosen X‑mask to themselves, begins to keep an onboarding diary according to the game rules, records the first steps. At some point, they stop perceiving the system as external and begin to act within it.

After passing the candidate minimum, the transition to the next level opens. The person gains access to more complex tools and becomes part of the institutional structure with full status of scenic citizenship.

---

## Why it works

Onboarding solves the problem that many projects face. People come, but do not understand the context and quickly lose interest.

Here, entry turns into a process of meaningful inclusion. The participant gradually masters the language of the project, begins to see relationships and feels their own role.

This creates sustainability. Instead of random users, participants appear who understand why they are here.

---

## Result

IMARCH GitChronical onboarding can be described as a transition protocol.

It turns registration into a cultural act.
It records the first steps and makes them part of the common chronicle.
It helps a person move from observation to action.

As a result, the system receives not just users, but subjects capable of participating in the development of a common institutional memory.

# 🔹 The meaning of onboarding and the result of passing

## What changes after passing onboarding

Onboarding is not a formal registration or a test of compliance. Its task is to form the subjectivity of the participant within the system.

After passing onboarding, the participant:

- receives a fixed role and the context of their participation
- creates their own chronicle of actions and decisions
- moves from the position of an observer to the position of an acting subject
- begins to interact with other participants through common protocols and language.

The main result is not a status, but a structured entry point into collective work.

---

## What this is NOT

To avoid false expectations, it is important to clarify the boundaries.

IMARCH GitChronical onboarding:

- is NOT an esoteric practice or belief system
- is NOT a closed club or social hierarchy
- does NOT replace professional training or competencies.

It is a tool for structuring participation and communication within an institutional environment.

---

## The institutional meaning of onboarding

Any sustainable system requires a common language and agreed rules of interaction.

Onboarding performs the function of a cultural synchronizer:

- creates a common context of understanding
- fixes communication rules
- ensures transparency of actions and decisions
- forms the basis for a collective chronicle.

Thus, onboarding is not an entry barrier, but a coordination mechanism that allows the system to work without chaos and mutual misunderstanding.
